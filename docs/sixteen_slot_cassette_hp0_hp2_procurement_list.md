## Purpose And Scope

This plan converts all 20 equipment gaps and all 21 physical hardware/article
gaps in LF-CAS-A7-RC001 into a prioritized, costed procurement program for one
FA-1 cassette campaign. It preserves the governing quantities, interfaces,
hold points, owners, and independent reviews. It does not approve a model,
supplier, substitution, pressure range, material, biological claim, or wet
test.

The budget covers the first-article equipment, contracted metrology, one
production-intent cassette set, the conservative four-single-use-harness path,
and the HP-1 qualification articles. It excludes internal engineering labor,
the D0-D9 drawing effort, role/reviewer fees, facility construction, sales tax,
E1 environmental-extension equipment, E2 hazardous-chemistry controls, live
cells or AAV, confirmation lots, and later production scale. EQ-12 includes the
base fluid-preparation set; the conditional viscosity-method add-on is shown
separately and is not in the core total.

Every row remains an UNVERIFIED GAP until its LF-CAS-A7-RC001 close action and
evidence package pass. A candidate family or budget allowance is not an exact
MPN. The selected usable range must avoid endpoint operation and saturation,
and the final measurement system must support the frozen uncertainty and
guard-band rules.

## Failure And Commercial Response

| Event | Procurement response |
| --- | --- |
| Quote exceeds the high allowance by >20% | Do not silently consume contingency. Rebid an identical controlled package, document scope differences, and obtain PR approval for a revised envelope. Acceptance criteria do not change to reduce price. |
| Quoted lead misses the critical path | Seek a second qualified source, rental, or contract-service path. Do not place an early production order with unreleased drawings. |
| Supplier refuses raw data, calibration identity, lot traceability, FAI, or change control | Disqualify the source for the controlled row even if price is lower. |
| Supplier proposes a model/material/process substitution | Quarantine the proposal. Reopen affected HP-0 selection, rating, compatibility, D7, drawing, calibration, and uncertainty reviews before any approval. |
| Received identity, quantity, lot, rating, certificate, or condition differs from the PO | Quarantine. Preserve packaging and records; issue an NCR; do not install, calibrate into the released system, or wet. |
| Calibration or control fails | Stop use and place dependent measurements on HOLD under the governing checklist. Repair/recalibrate and repeat affected evidence. |
| R2 custom article fails incoming inspection | Rework and reinspect to the released drawing, or prospectively revise/release the design and restart affected qualification. No averaging or acceptance-limit change. |
| HW-01 vendor or independent critical fails | Quarantine the complete matched set; follow LF-CAS-A7-RC001 HP-2 failure rules. Do not assemble around it or authorize additional sets. |
