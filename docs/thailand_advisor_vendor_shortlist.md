## Contact order

### 1. Thai Microelectronics Center (TMEC)

Best fit for the chip itself. TMEC describes itself as a specialty foundry that
helps industrial startups with design support, sensor prototypes, and volume
production. Its English microfluidics page explicitly offers design, prototype
manufacturing, and testing in silicon, PDMS, and PMMA. It lists soft
lithography, laser machining, oxygen plasma, surface modification, in-house
molds, an industrial cleanroom, and a stated prototype turnaround of two to
four weeks.

- Location: Chachoengsao, about one hour from Suvarnabhumi Airport according
  to TMEC's directions
- Contact: `info-tmec@nectec.or.th`
- Phone: +66 38 857 100-3; +66 92 687 1112
- Ask for: a paid English-language design review, PMMA process recommendation,
  bonding recommendation, and a quote for three Rev D0 coupons
- Confirm before paying: whether its stated feature range and turnaround apply
  to a 500 um wide by 200 um deep PMMA channel; whether it accepts an
  overseas customer without a Thai entity; day-rate or project minimum;
  measurement report; IP/NDA terms
- Sources: [TMEC microfluidics](https://www.tmec.or.th/microfluidics/),
  [TMEC contact](https://www.tmec.or.th/contact-us/)

### 2. Spot On CNC

Best conventional machining/DFM fallback. Its English site states that it
provides engineering, CNC machining, precision grinding, turning, laser
cutting, welding, assembly, 3D modeling, DFM, and prototype production. It also
says its engineers can help when a prototype design is incomplete.

- Location: Samut Prakan
- English contact: `export@spotoncnc.com`
- Phone: +66 2 030 0092
- Ask for: paid half-day DFM review, PMMA micro-milling capability, optical
  finish, flatness, deburring, inspection, and three-part prototype pricing
- Confirm before paying: PMMA experience; minimum tool diameter; reliable
  200 um depth control; whether it can bond or must hand off bonding; English
  engineer availability; customer visit policy
- Source: [Spot On CNC English site](https://spotoncnc.com/en/)

global product-development support, and its Asia site advertises prototyping
packages and 3D-printing/rapid-prototyping services. The corporate location
page lists ARRK Corporation (Thailand) in Bangkadi Industrial Park, Pathum
Thani.

- Location: Pathum Thani
- Phone: +66 2 501 1170
- Contact path: [ARRK Asia](https://asia.arrk.com/) or the phone above
- Ask for: an English mechanical DFM engineer, a paid workshop, a redlined

- Confirm before paying: PMMA micro-machining and bonding capability; whether
  the Thailand site performs the work; minimum project value; day-rate;
  200 um channel inspection; STEP/PDF deliverables
- Sources: [ARRK corporate site and Thailand location](https://www.arrk.com/),
  [ARRK Asia prototyping](https://asia.arrk.com/package-solutions-prototyping/)

## Advisor and introduction routes

### Thailand Science Park Scale X / general inquiry

Thailand Science Park's English inquiry form explicitly accepts United States
applicants and offers categories for research and innovation collaboration,
business incubation, facilities, and visits. TSP also announced Scale X as a
soft-landing program for Thai and international startups. This is the best
route for an introduction to a microfluidics advisor or a suitable company in
the park; it is not itself a fabrication quote.

- General/Scale X contact:
  [TSP international inquiry](https://www.sciencepark.or.th/en/tsp-scale-x-landing/contact-us)
- General phone: +66 2 564 7200 ext. 2
- Request: one paid English-speaking advisor, one TMEC/TSP introduction, and
  feedback on what evidence LaminarForge needs before a startup-program application
- Do not ask for: incorporation, lab lease, employees, or live-cell work yet

### iTAP eligibility warning

TSP's iTAP page offers expert matching and subsidized technical consultancy,
but its published applicant criteria require at least 51% Thai ownership. It
is therefore not the current funding route for LaminarForge. Its public expert
network may still be useful if TSP redirects the inquiry.

- Source: [TSP iTAP](https://www.sciencepark.or.th/en/innovation-technology-assistance-program-itap)

## Questions to send every candidate

1. Can we buy four to eight hours of an English-speaking engineer's time before
   ordering parts?
2. Will the engineer redline our STEP and drawing while Alex edits the design?
3. Can you machine or otherwise form PMMA channels approximately 500 um wide
   and 200 um deep?
4. What bonding method do you recommend for a transparent water/dye-only
   coupon, and how will you prevent channel collapse or adhesive intrusion?
5. What dimensions and surface properties can you inspect, with what report?
6. What are the minimum order, deposit, review price, prototype price, and lead
   time for three bonded coupons and one unbonded witness?
7. Will you return marked-up files and respect LaminarForge's IP/NDA terms?
8. May Alex attend as a customer to review parts and results without operating
   shop equipment?

## Go/no-go rule

Do not order fabrication from a candidate that will not first answer the
channel-depth, bonding, inspection, and English-engineer questions in writing.
