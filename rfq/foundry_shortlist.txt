================================================================================
              FOUNDRY SHORTLIST — IN713C Turbine Wheel
================================================================================

Target: Investment casting foundries with demonstrated IN713C / superalloy
turbine wheel capability. Prioritized by relevance to our part.

================================================================================
TIER 1 — SUPERALLOY TURBINE SPECIALISTS
================================================================================

1. PBS Velka Bites (Czech Republic)
   Website:  https://www.pbs.cz/en/Invesment-casting/Turbine-wheels
   Why:      Specialist in IN713C turbine wheels. Range: 50-400mm, 0.15-44 kg.
             Our 75mm wheel is their core business. They cast IN713C, IN713LC,
             B1914, MAR-M247. Full service: design, casting, machining, final
             product. Major turbocharger supplier (Siemens, MAN, etc.).
   Contact:  Ales Winkler, Sales — casting@pbs.cz, +420 566 822 403
   Notes:    European, may have longer shipping. Ask about MOQ and prototype
             pricing. They do SLA patterns for prototypes.

2. Aristo Cast (Almont, Michigan, USA)
   Website:  https://www.aristocast.com
   Why:      US-based, specializes in small precision castings including
             superalloys. Handles prototype quantities. Located in Michigan.
   Contact:  Request quote via website.
   Notes:    Verify IN713C capability (they may subcontract vacuum casting).
             Good for small prototype runs.

3. Barron Industries (Oxford, Michigan, USA)
   Website:  https://www.barron-industries.com
   Why:      Full-service investment casting, handles nickel superalloys.
             In-house heat treat, machining, and NDT. Michigan-based.
   Contact:  Request quote via website.
   Notes:    AS9100 certified (aerospace). May be more expensive for prototype
             but offers full traceability and quality documentation.

================================================================================
TIER 2 — GENERAL SUPERALLOY FOUNDRIES
================================================================================

4. Shellcast Foundries (Montreal, Canada)
   Website:  https://www.shellcast.com
   Why:      Premium investment casting. Detailed drawing notes template
             available (used as reference for our spec). Handles complex
             aerospace parts.
   Notes:    Primarily aluminum — verify superalloy capability.

5. Signicast (Hartford, Wisconsin, USA)
   Website:  https://www.signicast.com
   Why:      Large US investment casting house. High volume capability.
   Notes:    May not be cost-effective for 1-off prototype. Better for
             production quantities (50+).

6. BasTech (Dayton, Ohio, USA)
   Website:  https://bastech.com
   Why:      Integrates additive manufacturing with investment casting.
             Can do SLA patterns from STEP file for rapid prototyping.
             Published the 2026 Investment Casting Guide.
   Notes:    Good DFM collaboration. Ask about their rapid prototype program
             (2-3 week turnaround on first article).

================================================================================
TIER 3 — INTERNATIONAL OPTIONS (LOWER COST)
================================================================================

7. ZHY Casting (China)
   Website:  https://www.zhycasting.com
   Why:      Published the IN713C hot tearing study we referenced. Demonstrated
             capability with 86mm IN713C turbine wheels at 0.7mm blade thickness.
             They clearly know the metallurgy.
   Notes:    Lower cost but longer lead time and shipping. Quality documentation
             may need extra oversight. Good backup option.

8. Allied Casting (China)
   Website:  https://alliedcasting.com
   Why:      Active publisher of turbine blade casting content. Handles
             superalloy turbine components.
   Notes:    Same China-sourcing considerations as above.

================================================================================
RECOMMENDATION
================================================================================

Start with PBS (Tier 1, #1) — they are the closest match to our exact part.
Send the RFQ email + STEP + spec to PBS first. If pricing is too high or lead
time too long, follow up with Aristo Cast and Barron Industries (US-based).

For fastest prototype turnaround, BasTech's SLA-pattern-to-casting approach
may be worth exploring in parallel (no hard tooling = 2-3 weeks).
