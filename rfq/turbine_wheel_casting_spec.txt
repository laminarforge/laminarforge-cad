1. SCOPE
================================================================================

This specification defines the requirements for investment casting of a radial
inflow turbine wheel for a hydrogen-fueled turbogenerator. The part operates at
95,000 RPM and 850 deg C inlet temperature. It is a single-piece integral wheel
(blades + hub + back disk) with no inserts or joints.

================================================================================

================================================================================
5. CASTING NOTES
================================================================================

1. This part is designed for INVESTMENT CASTING ONLY. Do not CNC from billet.
   Inconel 713C is a cast-only alloy (not wrought).

2. Shrinkage: Foundry to apply appropriate shrinkage factor for IN713C
   (typically ~2.0-2.2%). Foundry determines exact value based on their process.

3. Gating and risers: Foundry to design gating system. Center-pour through
   bore or bottom-pour through back disk face recommended. Blade tips are
   the highest hot-tearing risk area per ProCAST simulation literature.

4. Blade root fillets: The STEP model includes R0.4 mm fillets at blade-hub
   junctions. Foundry may increase fillet radii up to R0.8 mm if their
   process simulation indicates hot tearing risk. Do NOT eliminate fillets.

5. All internal corners and transitions shall have radii (no sharp corners).
   External corners: 0.3 mm max break.

6. Weld repair is NOT permitted on blades or bore. Minor weld repair on
   back disk OD is acceptable per AMS 5391.

7. Parting line flash and gate witness marks shall not exceed 0.25 mm.
   No gate remnants on blade surfaces.

8. Casting shall be free of visual cracks, laps, cold shuts, and inclusions
   in blade and bore areas.

================================================================================
6. POST-CAST PROCESSING
================================================================================

6.1 HOT ISOSTATIC PRESSING (HIP)
    Required. Per AMS 2175 or foundry standard practice.
    Typical: 1200 deg C, 100 MPa argon, 4 hours.
    Purpose: Close internal microporosity.

6.2 HEAT TREATMENT
    IN713C is normally used AS-CAST (no solution treatment needed).
    If foundry recommends solution + age for improved properties:
      Solution: 2150 deg F (1177 deg C) for 2 hours, vacuum, air cool
      Stabilize: 1700 deg F (927 deg C) for 16 hours, air cool
    Foundry to advise based on their experience with 713C.

6.3 MACHINING (by foundry or customer — to be determined)
    A. Bore: Ream or hone to 12.0 H7 (+0.000/+0.018 mm)
    B. Tip OD: Turn to 75.0 +/-0.15 mm, concentric to bore
    C. Back face: Face to perpendicularity, establish balance datum

6.4 BALANCING
    Balance to ISO 1940 Grade G2.5 at 95,000 RPM.
    Material removal from back disk face only (no blade grinding).
    Residual unbalance: max 0.02 g-mm.
    Balancing may be performed by customer if foundry does not offer.

7. INSPECTION AND QUALITY
================================================================================

7.1 DIMENSIONAL
    First article: Full CMM inspection of all critical dimensions (Section 4).
    Production: Sample inspection per agreed AQL.
    CMM report required with first article.

7.2 MATERIAL
    Material test report (MTR) / mill certification per heat.
    Chemical analysis per AMS 5391.
    Mechanical properties (tensile test bar cast with the part).

7.3 NON-DESTRUCTIVE TESTING
    A. Visual inspection: 100% of parts, all surfaces.
       Accept/reject per AMS 5391 and ASTM E155 (reference radiographs).

    B. Fluorescent penetrant inspection (FPI): 100% of parts.
       Per ASTM E1417. No linear indications on blades or bore.

    C. Radiographic inspection: First article only (or per foundry recommendation).
       Per ASTM E2422 or AMS 2175.
       Level 2 acceptance on blades, Level 3 on hub.

7.4 DOCUMENTATION DELIVERABLES
    With each shipment:
      - Material test report (chemistry + mechanical properties)
      - CMM dimensional report (first article)
      - FPI report
      - Certificate of conformance
      - Radiographic film/digital images (first article)

8. PACKAGING AND SHIPPING
================================================================================

Parts shall be individually wrapped in VCI paper or equivalent corrosion
inhibitor. Blades shall be protected from contact damage. Parts shall be
packed in rigid containers to prevent damage during shipping.
